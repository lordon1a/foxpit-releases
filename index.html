FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something missing? Ask us on GitHub.

Do I need my own AI subscription?

Yes. Foxpit runs the agents you already have — Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Antigravity — with your own accounts. The built-in Foxpit engine works with any model provider you connect.

Does Foxpit see or upload my code?

No. Your code, prompts and terminal output stay on your computer. Agent events travel over 127.0.0.1 only. Foxpit contacts its account server to check your plan, and GitHub to check for updates. That is all.

Do I need an account?

Yes, a free one. Sign in with an email code, Google or GitHub to start your 3-day trial. No card needed.

What happens after the 3-day trial?

You will need Foxpit Pro to keep using the app. Pro is not open yet: until it is, Foxpit keeps working after your trial.

Does it change how my agents work?

No. Foxpit adds standard hooks so agents can report what they are doing. You can remove them with one click from the Agents panel.

Do I have to use the mascots?

No. Turn the office off in Settings and Foxpit becomes a clean multi-terminal and chat cockpit.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11, macOS on Apple Silicon, and Linux (AppImage). Node.js is not required.
